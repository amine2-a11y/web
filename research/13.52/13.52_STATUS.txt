HEN 13.52 TEST-SAFE
====================

Firmware profile: FW_13.52_EXPERIMENTAL
Public offsets integrated:
- PRISON0  = 0x0111FA18
- ROOTVNODE= 0x02136E90
- SYSENT    = 0x01102B70

TEST MODE:
13.52 is routed through the experimental profile, but the kernel-exploit
stage is gated unless a verified 13.52 exploit primitive and matching
shellcode are present. This prevents the older Lapse/Poops chain from being
run with incompatible 13.52 assumptions.

Payload-only fallback remains available at:
/disc/BDMV/AUXDATA/hen.bin

This package is for testing the 13.52 profile/loader integration. It does
not claim a complete, hardware-verified 13.52 jailbreak chain.
